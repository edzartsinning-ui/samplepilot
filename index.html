Understand your audio

44.1 kHz. 96 kHz. 192 kHz.
See what your DAC uses.

A Hi-Res music file and the actual output format of your Mac are not necessarily the same thing.

Important distinction: SamplePilot displays and manages supported audio output formats. The DAC's configured sample rate does not prove the original quality of the music source.

Automatic track-format switching currently relies on available Apple Music metadata. Streaming metadata is not independently verified. Other music players can use manually selected output rates, but universal automatic track-format detection is not available.

Bit-perfect playback cannot be guaranteed.
